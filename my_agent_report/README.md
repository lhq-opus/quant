# Agent Adoption and Delivery: Lessons for AI Decision-Making

This project develops an English report for leadership on lessons from agent adoption and project delivery at miHoYo, including the author's work in its data team. Experience at ByteDance, an earlier employer, provides comparative context. Its purpose is to inform AI decisions at the author's current company. A corresponding Chinese version is maintained so the author can check for omissions or inaccurate wording.

Read the [working report](report.md).

[Chinese reading guide](README.zh-CN.md) · [Chinese report](report.zh-CN.md)

**Status:** Early working draft. Material covers miHoYo's November–December 2025 adoption baseline, the author's data-team project, Dify's limited attention, and a December discussion with a senior leader about a general-purpose agent platform running on servers, a skill hub, and knowledge bases. The discussion's goals were to help employees move their business workflows onto the platform or use it for end-to-end work; approval and implementation remain unconfirmed. ByteDance provides comparative context from late 2023 to the end of 2025. These organizational accounts are attributed to the author and have not been independently corroborated here. Claude Sonnet 4.5's release date has been checked against Anthropic's announcement. The full review period, detailed case outcomes, decision rationale, and recommendations remain open.

**Language consistency:** The English and Chinese reports and reading guides must remain aligned in structure, facts, numbers, dates, sources, evidence status, conclusions, recommendations, uncertainties, and open information gaps. Additions, deletions, clarifications, and corrections in either language must be reflected in both within the same update and published in the same Git commit. Both versions are reviewed section by section before each commit and again after rebasing, before pushing.

The report will connect experiences across stages and types of agent application, covering successful, unsuccessful, and mixed outcomes as the evidence develops. It will trace the company's attitudes toward AI, decisions, and organizational reflection over time, alongside the author's personal views. The chronology follows the events described, rather than the order in which material is received; later stages and application categories will be developed from further material. Company positions and reflection are distinguished from the author's observations and judgments. Reported experience, publicly verified facts, interpretation, and recommendations are identified separately, with links to public sources where available.

The deliverable is standard Markdown prepared for transfer to Confluence. Git publication does not publish a Confluence page.
